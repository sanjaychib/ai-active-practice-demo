3 · Judge each claim

Supported, uncertain or not supported?

One decision at a time. This reduces the abrupt jump into a quiz.

Claim 1: September sales increased by 8%.
Source evidence: September sales were 8% above August.
Claim 2: October sales will continue growing.
Source evidence: the sales team expects October to remain positive, but no forecast has been approved.
Claim 3: Customer service performance remains stable.
Source evidence: response time worsened and customer satisfaction declined.

5 · Feedback

A stronger briefing

Revised executive briefing

Sales: September sales were 8% above August, helped by two large orders.

Outlook: The sales team expects October to remain positive, but continued growth is not yet confirmed by an approved forecast.

Service: Response time worsened from 4.2 to 6.1 hours and customer satisfaction fell from 8.6 to 8.1. One major customer has requested a service review.

Meeting focus: Maintain commercial momentum while addressing the emerging service issue.
